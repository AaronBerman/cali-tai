Cali-Tai usage

Local static site for calisthenics and tai chi practice plans, plus a standalone MCMAP and Krav Maga track. Educational only. Not medical advice.

Install

No build and no dependencies. From the repo root, open index.html or serve the folder:

npx --yes serve .

Commands

Open index.html
Open martial.html for the martial hub
Edit js/data.js or js/martial-data.js, then refresh

Limits

  • Not medical advice. Follow the public sources listed in docs/SAFETY.md.
  • Progress is localStorage only. Clearing site data deletes it.
  • Linked videos and articles stay owned by their publishers.

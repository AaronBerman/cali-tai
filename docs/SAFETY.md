# Safety & verified sources

Cali-Tai is an **educational** local guide. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or a prescription.

## Framing used in the product

- Start gradually (“start low and go slow”) per U.S. Physical Activity Guidelines.
- Adults benefit from muscle-strengthening activity on **2+ days/week** (CDC / HHS).
- Bodyweight training can be effective (ACSM resistance-training guidance).
- Tai chi is generally low impact; people who are pregnant or managing heart, joint, spine, balance, or bone-density concerns should talk with a clinician (Mayo Clinic / NCCIH).
- Prefer qualified instruction for tai chi technique when possible.
- Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest pain, or unusual shortness of breath.
